Why I Believe Cultural Heritage is Important

Cultural heritage shapes how we understand ourselves, our communities, and the world around us. I chose to take this class because I want to explore the traditions, stories, and historical experiences that influence identity—both my own and others’. Learning about cultural heritage helps me see how different cultures preserve meaning, memory, and values across generations.

What Cultural Heritage Means to Me

To me, cultural heritage is more than artifacts or old buildings—it's the living expression of people’s experiences. It includes music, food, language, rituals, and many more things that carry meaning and helps us appreciate the other cultures of the world.

A Cultural Heritage Site That Inspires Me

One site that inspires me is the Great Wall of China. It represents centuries of history, engineering, and cultural identity. As a kid, what captivated me the most about it was not only its size, but the amount of time, people, and energy it took to create such a thing.

What I Hope to Learn

Through this class, I hope to learn how cultural heritage is preserved, how it changes over time, and how communities protect it. I’m also interested in understanding how digital tools, museums, and archives help keep cultural memory alive for future generations.
